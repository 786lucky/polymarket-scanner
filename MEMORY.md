## 🔑 KEY FORMULAS WE LIVE BY

### Bayes Theorem (parlex_exe)
posterior_odds = prior_odds × likelihood_ratio
Edge = model_probability - market_probability
If edge > threshold → TRADE. Otherwise → WAIT.

### Polymarket Fee Model (exact)
fee = (bps/10000) × min(price, 1-price) × shares

### Stoikov Market Making (0XDegen)
r = s − qγσ²(T − t)
Reservation price adjusts for inventory risk

### Time Awareness Decay
adjusted_prob = 0.5 + (raw_prob - 0.5) × (remaining_minutes / window_minutes)
Less time = less confidence = shrink toward 50%

### Phase-Based Decision Gates
EARLY (>10 min): 5% edge minimum, 55% probability minimum
MID (5-10 min): 10% edge minimum, 60% probability minimum
LATE (<5 min): 20% edge minimum, 65% probability minimum
